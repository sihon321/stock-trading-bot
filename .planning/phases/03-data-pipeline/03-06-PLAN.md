| Source adapters to orchestrator | External data health decisions are combined into final context policy. |
| DataSource to future LLM/execution | Compact context can influence future signals and orders. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-06-T | Tampering | `MarketDataSource` | critical | mitigate | Resolve every unhealthy required source to `SKIP_CANDIDATE` or `FORCE_HOLD` before emitting `DataContext`. |
| T-03-06-R | Repudiation | `DataSourceAuditEvent` | high | mitigate | Preserve ticker, source, reason, observed date/time, expected date, and action in audit events and visible warnings. |
| T-03-06-I | Information Disclosure | Compact `DataContext` | medium | mitigate | Keep raw source metadata, tokens, headers, and vendor payloads out of `DataContext`. |
| T-03-06-E | Elevation of Privilege | Core ports | medium | mitigate | Keep `ports.py` adapter-free and synchronous; concrete adapters are opt-in through `data_source.py`. |
</threat_model>
